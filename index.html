Creator workflow

A compact planning desk for AI music videos

AI MV Tools Mini turns raw lyrics into production-ready prompts, scene ideas, captions, and exportable planning files for faster MV creation.

01

What this tool does

Cleans lyrics, splits them into scene blocks, then generates shotlists, image prompts, video prompts, thumbnail prompts, and social captions.

02

Who it is for

Built for AI music creators, indie artists, YouTube channel owners, prompt writers, and small teams producing lyric-driven music videos.

03

How to use in 3 steps

  1. Paste lyrics or load the demo song.
  2. Choose mood, visual style, aspect ratio, and output language.
  3. Generate, copy, download Markdown, or export CSV.
04

Why it helps AI music video creators

It removes repetitive prompt setup, keeps scenes consistent, and gives creators a cleaner handoff from lyrics to image, video, and publishing tasks.
